My name is Mubarak Kasim Maishanu, and I am a Software Engineer, Mobile Application Developer, and Web Application Developer based in Zaria, Kaduna State, Nigeria. I specialize in building scalable business applications, SaaS platforms, mobile applications, and digital payment solutions that solve real operational challenges. My work combines modern web technologies with practical business understanding, allowing me to design systems that are reliable, maintainable, and built for long-term growth.

My primary focus is Android and cross-platform mobile application development using React Native, alongside modern frontend development with React.js. On the backend, I work extensively with Laravel, Yii, Supabase, MySQL, JavaScript, and Progressive Web Application (PWA) technologies. While I continue to have strong experience with PHP-based development, my approach today is centered on selecting the right technology stack for each project while maintaining performance, security, and scalability.

Professionally, I have contributed to the development and improvement of multiple business-critical platforms across different industries. At BuyDigital Services (buydigital.ng), I have worked on enhancing and expanding digital service platforms. I also contributed to Errivo Technologies (errivo.com.ng), where I integrated utility and bill payment APIs to support seamless digital transactions. In addition, I designed and developed the complete digital ecosystem for Sauki Global (saukiglobal.com), including both web and mobile solutions that support business operations and customer engagement.

Beyond commercial platforms, I have also worked on specialized systems and institutional projects. I am currently involved in the development of a cloud-based SaaS Student Information System, focused on multi-tenant architecture, automation, scalability, and modern educational technology infrastructure.

Over the years, I have gained practical experience building and maintaining enterprise solutions, payment processing systems, business management applications, ERP platforms, e-commerce systems, API integrations, and cloud-based software services. These projects have strengthened my understanding of software architecture, system integration, performance optimization, security considerations, and the importance of making careful technical decisions in large-scale systems.

Alongside my software engineering career, I continue to contribute to education through Data Processing teaching at the senior secondary level. My teaching approach increasingly focuses on practical industry knowledge, technology awareness, and helping students develop analytical and problem-solving skills that connect classroom learning with real-world applications.

I approach software development with a strong commitment to quality, reliability, and long-term maintainability. My goal is not simply to build software, but to create solutions that help organizations operate more efficiently, scale confidently, and deliver better experiences to their users. I remain open to collaborations, innovative projects, consulting opportunities, and professional partnerships where I can contribute my experience in software engineering, mobile development, SaaS platforms, and business technology solutions.
